Highlights:
- Official EV Tech Services logo shown in full, without redesign or cropping.
- Premium dark navy / white / green engineering aesthetic.
- Responsive desktop and mobile layout.

- Site inspection, load assessment, installation, smart setup, troubleshooting, maintenance and support sections.

- Compatibility / multi-brand support messaging.

- Contact form opens WhatsApp with the submitted details.

Research direction:
The information architecture was inspired by recurring patterns on leading EV charging platforms such as ABB E-mobility, ChargePoint, Wallbox and Tesla: use-case segmentation, site assessment, end-to-end deployment, software/connectivity, maintenance/support, customer education and clear expert-contact CTAs. No third-party branding or website copy is reproduced.
