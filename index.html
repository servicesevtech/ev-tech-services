EV knowledge

Questions customers ask before they install.

Useful information can make the first conversation much easier — especially when charger wiring depends on the actual site.

Why can't I get one fixed wiring price over the phone?

Because EV charger wiring depends on the existing electrical setup, cable length and route, DB condition, charger requirements, parking location and other site-specific factors. A site assessment gives a more meaningful quotation.

Do I need a site inspection before installing an EV charger?

A site inspection is useful for confirming the electrical and physical requirements before work begins, particularly where the charger is a higher-load appliance.

Can you work with a charger I already purchased?

Yes, subject to the charger’s specifications, condition and suitability for the site. Share the model or photos so the installation requirements can be reviewed.

Do you provide support after installation?

Yes. EV Tech Services provides troubleshooting, maintenance and technical support for EV charging systems.
